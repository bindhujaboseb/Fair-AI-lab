🧠 AI learns from data
An AI only knows what is in its training examples.
⚠️ Bias = unfair pattern
When a group is missing or has too few examples, the AI works badly for that group.
🌍 Fair & inclusive AI
Collect balanced data from everyone and test the AI on every group.
